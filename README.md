🎓 Hi, I'm John Santos, an Electrical Engineer with a 
passion for building things that bring hardware and 
software together. I graduated from the University 
of Calgary in 2024 with a BSc in Electrical 
Engineering and a minor in Digital Engineering.

🤖 I'm particularly interested in embedded systems, 
machine learning, and robotics. What I enjoy most is 
taking an idea from the drawing board and turning it 
into something real—designing a PCB, writing embedded 
software, deploying a machine learning model, or 
bringing a robotic system to life. I like working 
across the boundary between hardware and software, 
where different pieces have to come together to make 
a system actually work.

⚙️ I place a strong emphasis on quality and attention 
to detail in my work. I believe thoughtful engineering 
comes from questioning design decisions, exploring 
ways to improve and optimize a system, and understanding 
how each decision contributes to the overall result. 
I enjoy brainstorming, iterating, and refining designs 
to make them more efficient, reliable, functional, 
and purposeful.

🚀 I'm always looking for new challenges, new things to 
learn, and new ideas to bring to life. Explore some 
of my projects to see what I've been building along 
the way.

📫 You can reach me through this email johnmarisantos@protonmail.com

## Deploy

You can deploy the website portfolio through a local webserver using Python.

```shell
python -m http.server 8000
```

Enter the link in your browser.

```
http://localhost:8000/index.html
```
